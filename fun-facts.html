FUNFACTS

Maria used to work at the port... until her contract ended.

Six months later, Evros arrived and took her position.

Yes... her job first.

Even though Evros knew of Maria, they had never actually met.

Two years later, a colleague happened to mention that Evros had taken Maria’s old position at the port. That conversation finally led to their first meeting!

Apparently, this was the world’s slowest job handover.

On 21 September 2023, after a jet ski ride, they officially became a couple.

Because normal people just get coffee.

About a year later, they decided to build a life together. They moved into their home on 5 May 2025.

In March 2026, Evros decided it was time to get the ring.

On 15 July, Maria’s birthday, just before their summer holidays, he surprised her with the proposal!

First he took her job. Then he took her heart.

Thank you for celebrating with us!

EVROS & MARIA · 10.06.2028

A little story with a happy ending... and an even better beginning.
